### 1. [STING reprogramming by estrogen promotes neutrophil extracellular trap formation and sexbiased resistance to ADC therapy](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.09.031)
- **期刊**：cell ｜ **日期**：2026-11-19
- **匹配**：肿瘤免疫（标题：neutrophil extracellular）、抗肿瘤药物（摘要：trastuzumab）、cGAS-STING通路（标题：sting） ｜ **得分**：21

> Sex differences in cancer therapy response remain poorly understood. Using a ‘‘phase II-like preclinical trial’’ across 55 CLDN18.2+ pancreatic cancer patient-derived xenografts, we identify marked female-biased resistance to CLDN18.2-targeted antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) therapy despite equivalent antigen expression. Mechanistically, estrogen signals through GPER1 to upregulate TRAF6, redirecting tumor-intrinsic STING signaling from IRF3-mediated interferon production toward nuclear factor κB (NF-κB) activation. This drives CXCL1 secretion, neutrophil recruitment, and neutrophil extracellular trap (NET) formation. NETs form a charged extracellular network that restricts antibody access to tumor-cell-surface antigens—a mechanism we term functional antigen shielding—reducing ADC cytotoxicity independently of antigen expression. The NET burden also correlated with poorer responses to trastuzumab, cetuximab, and enfortumab vedotin across three clinical cohorts. Pharmacologic NET disruption with sivelestat restored ADC efficacy in female mice. These findings reveal an estrogen-driven resistance mechanism with broad implications for antibodybased cancer therapy.
